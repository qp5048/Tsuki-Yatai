# Valvskolan (skolan/) — surface brief

Scope: multi-page site for a fictional Stockholm grundskola (F–9). Mode: Persuade on the home page, Operate on timetable, lunch and calendar pages.
Audience: parents and pupils checking everyday info; prospective parents; portfolio reviewers.
Action: see today's lessons and lunch for your class (home live board), book a school visit.
Constraints: English, fictional and labelled, "Made by Zakharii", back link to hub, static, WCAG 2.1 AA.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The school is a Stockholm tunnelbana station: every page is a whitewashed rock cavern painted by a different artist, and practical information is set as station signage. Refuses the white page with a photo hero and a row of cards.
OWN-WORLD: rough whitewashed bedrock ground (cool white, generative rock relief), each page painted in one station palette (cobalt vines; spruce and signal red; night navy with gold; sky blue with arcs), year groups as metro lines (green F–3, red 4–6, blue 7–9), station-name plates, a dark departure board with amber figures, Familjen Grotesk.
STORY: Visitors learn Valvskolan is a lively F–9 school under the city's rock, see their class's day at once, then explore timetable, lunch, calendar and news, and book a visit.
FIRST VIEWPORT: a carved rock vault arch spans the viewport, cobalt vines painted across its walls; the school name as a station plate top-left; centre-right a live departure board "Next for class 5B" with lessons, today's lunch and next event, class switcher on the board; primary button Book a visit beneath the name.
FORM: grounded candidate 6 of 7 (Stockholm metro art), seed key 5be31e3e, degraded roll, code-led.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
