# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, no build step, deployed on GitHub Pages from the qp5048/Tsuki-Yatai repository (confirmed by the existing projects in this repo). Each project lives in its own folder beside a shared hub page (`index.html`).

## Users
This repository is Zakharii's web portfolio. The real audience is people evaluating Zakharii's front-end and design skills: potential employers and small-business clients. Each project is a fictional business website built as a showcase.

The third project (`skolan/` route, name to be decided in design) is a fictional grundskola (compulsory school, ages 6–16, förskoleklass to year 9) in Stockholm. Its in-world users are parents of current and prospective pupils, and pupils themselves, checking practical everyday information.

## Product Purpose
Show that Zakharii can build a larger multi-page site with working interactive features, quality design and accessibility, not only a one-page landing.

For the school site, confirmed priority features: a weekly timetable per class and the school lunch menu for the week (Schedule & lunch), and an events calendar with filters plus a news feed (Calendar & news). Several pages with working tabs.

## Positioning
A portfolio piece judged on craft: design suited to a modern school in Sweden / Stockholm, and functionality that actually works rather than placeholders.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- All content in English (consistent with the other portfolio projects).
- A "Made by Zakharii" credit on every page and a link back to the hub ("All projects"); the hub page gets a third entry for this site.
- No backend: interactivity runs in the browser; anything stored lives in localStorage and is labelled as a demo.
- Everything about the school is fictional and must be labelled as such (name, staff, address, events, menus).

## Evidence on Hand
No real school content, photos or staff exist. Photos may come from Unsplash with credit. No real Stockholm school may be imitated; no invented claims presented as real (rankings, inspection results, statistics).

## Product Principles
- Practical first: a parent should find today's lunch, this week's timetable and the next event in seconds.
- Working, not decorative: every tab, filter and control does its job.
- Honest fiction: clearly a showcase, clearly invented.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Swedish public-sector sites follow WCAG 2.1 AA (DOS law); the showcase should meet it: keyboard-operable tabs, readable contrast, reduced motion respected, works on phones.
