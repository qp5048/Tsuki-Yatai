Our school

Who we are, who works here, how to find us.

A school inside a mountain

In 1962 the city blasted a civil defence shelter into the rock under Klippgränd. Sixty years later we turned it into a school: the classrooms sit in the old building above, and the dining hall, library and sports hall are in the vaults below.

Like the metro stations nearby, the bare rock walls are painted. Every year one class designs a new section, so the school slowly becomes the largest drawing any of our pupils will ever make.

We are a municipal school with 412 pupils from reception class (förskoleklass) to year 9, an after-school club for the youngest, and a kitchen that cooks everything from scratch.

  • Outside every day

    Every class spends time outdoors daily, in the schoolyard, in Vita Bergen park or further out on trips.

  • Calm to learn

    Phones rest in lockers during the day, and the library has a quiet room for anyone who needs one.

  • Everyone's wall

    Pupils paint the rock and pick the art. The school is theirs, and it looks like it.
