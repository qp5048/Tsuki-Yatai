    Three lines, one school

    We split the school the way Stockholm splits its metro. Each line has its own teachers, schoolyard and rhythm, and they all meet at lunch.

    1. F123

      F–3Green line: learning to read the world

      Reading every morning, maths with your hands, and at least one lesson outdoors each day. The after-school club opens at 06:30 and closes at 17:30.

    2. 456

      4–6Red line: big projects, own ideas

      Woodwork, music and a makerspace with 3D printers. Each term ends with a project the class presents to their families.

    3. 789

      7–9Blue line: ready for upper secondary

      Subject teachers, a second modern language, work experience in year 8 and calm, steady support through the national tests in year 9.
